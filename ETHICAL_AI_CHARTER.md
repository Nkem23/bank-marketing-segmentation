# Ethical AI Charter

Project: Customer Segmentation and Ethical AI for Term-Deposit Outreach (BAN6800)

## Vision

To improve outreach efficiency without treating customers unfairly, compromising privacy or removing human accountability. The model supports decisions and does not make consequential decisions autonomously.

## Principles

1. Fairness: evaluate outcomes across age, marital status and education, and disclose the missing gender data.
2. Transparency: explain how segments, predictions and outreach recommendations are generated.
3. Privacy: minimise data access and apply appropriate security and retention controls.
4. Accountability: keep human decision-makers responsible for outreach policies and model use.
5. Responsible use: prohibit unapproved use of outreach scores for credit decisions or coercive customer treatment, and review the system when material risks emerge.

## Oversight

A proposed AI Ethics and Data Governance Committee, with business, analytics, compliance, privacy and risk representatives, reviews fairness, privacy, misuse and deployment readiness. It can require corrective action or recommend that deployment be paused if material risks remain unresolved. The data protection officer and compliance team advise on legal requirements.

## Fairness objectives

- Initial target: each age band's recall stays within 10 percentage points of overall recall. This is a screening threshold, not proof of fairness.
- Report group sizes, confidence intervals, recall and selection rates for age, marital status and education.
- Gender fairness cannot be directly assessed because the dataset has no gender column.
